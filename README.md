
# US SuperStore Profitability Analysis

## Overview
Analysis of 9,945 retail transactions from a US superstore covering 
3 product categories, 17 sub-categories, and 4 regional markets. 
Built using Power BI.

## Key Findings
- Technology leads in both sales ($835K) and profit ($145K) 
  with a 17.4% margin
- Furniture generates strong sales ($740K) but only $18K profit — 
  a margin of just 2.5%
- Tables sub-category loses $17,726 despite $207K in sales — 
  the store loses money on every Table sold
- Copiers and Paper are the hidden stars — profit margins of 
  37% and 43% respectively
- Central region underperforms at 7.9% margin vs West at 14.9%
- Total revenue: $2.3M | Overall profit margin: 12.47%

## Tools Used
Power BI, Excel, DAX measures

## Data Source
Sample Superstore dataset — 9,945 transactions across US regions
